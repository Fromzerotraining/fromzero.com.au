What we work on

Make the team
next time.

Missing out stings. Rep Ready turns it into a plan, building the skills selectors look for so you walk into the next tryout ready.

01

Skills

Ball handling, shooting and finishing, built up properly with game-speed reps.

02

Decisions

Reading the play and making the right read, the way rep coaches expect.

03

Competing

Live, competitive play against players at your level, every week.

04

Confidence

Turning practice into belief, so you play your best when it counts.

How it works

Court + gym.

Skills get you noticed, but selectors also pick the players who are quicker to the ball, stronger through contact and first off the floor. Every Rep Ready session pairs basketball work on court with strength and movement training, so players get faster, stronger and more explosive.

01

Strength training

Age-appropriate strength work in our gym, coached technique first.

  • Stronger legs, hips and core
  • Hold your ground and finish through contact
  • More power behind every jump and first step
02

Movement training

Speed, agility and jumping, taught the way basketball is played.

  • Acceleration and a quicker first step
  • Stopping, cutting and changing direction
  • Jumping higher and landing safely

Missed out on a rep team?

Don't wait.
Get ready.

Rep Ready is for U12 to U18 players who missed out on a rep team this season, or who want to give themselves the best shot at the next tryout. The players who make it are the ones who keep working.

Sessions are run by the From Zero coaching team, led by Emmanuel Malou, who played professionally for 12 years, and Jimmy, who has spent 12 years coaching players from grassroots through to the pros.

  • Structured sessions every Friday night
  • Coaching on the skills selectors look for
  • Strength and movement training in our gym
  • Competitive reps against players your age
