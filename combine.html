The tour

Three cities.
Top competition.

The top 10 players from the Combine are travelling to China with the From Zero coaching staff. They'll compete against the best young players in the country, from the same programs that have produced NBA and CBA professionals.

Flights, accommodation, meals and competition are all covered by From Zero. Each player also comes home with a professional player profile.

Stop 1Shanghai
Stop 2Beijing
Stop 3Guangzhou

How players were selected

Measured, then tested live.

The Combine is an NBA draft-style assessment. Every player went through the same physical testing and on-court evaluation.

01

Force plates

VALD testing for explosive power, reactive strength and left/right asymmetry.

02

Vertical jump

Countermovement and approach jumps, measured precisely.

03

Sprint & agility

Acceleration, linear speed and change of direction.

04

On-court

Skills, decision making and competitive play, assessed by our coaches.
