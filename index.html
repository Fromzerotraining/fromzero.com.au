What we do best

Scorers are built, not born.

When the play breaks down and the shot clock is running out, someone on the floor has to go and get a bucket.

Building that player is what we do best. We train the physical qualities and the basketball skills that scorers rely on, and we train them together. A great handle means very little if you can't hold your ground through contact. A strong, explosive body only becomes a weapon when it comes with the skill and decision making to use it.

What makes a scorer

Six things.
We train all of them.

Every scorer we've worked with, from juniors to NBL, NBA and CBA players, relies on the same six qualities. Some get built in the gym, some on the court and most need both.

Gym
01

Strength

Scorers are strong enough to absorb contact, hold their line to the rim and finish through a defender's body. We build that strength in the gym with programs designed for basketball.

Gym + Court
02

Stopping on a dime

Decelerating and changing direction at speed is what creates separation. We train the braking qualities in the body and the footwork on the court that turn a hard drive into an open look.

Court
03

Handle

Scorers get where they want to go with the ball under pressure. Our skill sessions build a handle that holds up against real defence, not one that only looks good in the warm-up.

Court
04

Shooting

We work on mechanics, footwork and shot preparation until a player's shot is repeatable off the catch, off the dribble and off movement.

Gym + Court
05

Finishing

Scorers finish over and around any defender. That takes vertical power, body control and a wide range of finishes, which we develop on the court and in the gym.

Court
06

Decision making

The best scorers make the right read under pressure: when to attack, when to pull up and when to make the extra pass. We build that game understanding into every session.

Measured & protected

No guessing.
No time lost.

You can't improve what you don't measure, and you can't score from the sideline. Testing and physio keep every athlete on plan and on the floor.

Athletic Testing

Each athlete gets a clear picture of where they stand on vertical jump, speed, power and strength. Coaches use it to write programs that target that athlete's needs. Retesting shows exactly how far you've come and keeps athlete and coach accountable to the plan.
