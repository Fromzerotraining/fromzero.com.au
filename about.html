About From Zero

Built from zero.

We're a basketball performance facility in Keilor Park, Melbourne. We develop complete players: skill on the court, strength in the gym, testing and physio, all under one roof.

Our story

The name is
the philosophy.

Scorers aren't born. They're built, one rep, one lift and one session at a time, usually when nobody is watching.

From Zero started with a simple idea. Players shouldn't have to piece their development together from a skills coach in one suburb, a gym in another and a physio who has never spoken to either. Basketball players need all of it, working together, pointed at the same goal.

So we built it in one place. Our basketball sessions, strength and conditioning, athletic testing and sports physio all run out of our Keilor Park facility. Coaches and physio share the same information and the same plan for each athlete.

Some athletes come to us starting from nothing. Some are already good, but good isn't enough anymore. Wherever you're starting from, we meet you there, measure it and build from it. No gimmicks and no shortcuts, just hard-earned progress and the compound effect of showing up.

Led by experience

24 years of the game.

One of us played it at the professional level for 12 years. The other has spent 12 years coaching athletes from their first session through to the pros. Between them, they know what it takes to become a scorer.

12 years as a pro

Emmanuel Malou

Head of Basketball Performance

Emmanuel played professionally for 12 years, including the NBL and international basketball with Team South Sudan. He brings what he learned at that level to every court session: how scorers move, read the defence and get their shot off.

12 years coaching

Jimmy Mullins

Founder · Director · High Performance Coach

Jimmy has spent 12 years coaching basketball athletes from grassroots through to NBL, NBA and CBA pros, in Australia and China. That experience shapes everything we do at From Zero: results first, everything tracked, and nothing in a program just because it looks good.
