What You'll Work On

Two Skills.
Two Hours.

Every session splits across the two areas that separate good players from the ones who take over — a repeatable shot and the speed to create space. No standing around, just focused reps with eyes on every one.

Shooting
A Shot You
Can Trust

Build a clean, repeatable jumper that holds up under pressure — from the mechanics up.

  • Mechanics — base, alignment, release and follow-through dialled in
  • Footwork — setting feet off the catch and off the dribble
  • Shooting Off The Catch & Dribble — game-realistic entries
  • Range Extension — adding distance without breaking form
  • Shooting Under Fatigue — knocking shots down at game speed
  • Free Throws & Routine — a consistent pre-shot routine that travels
Speed
First To
Every Spot

Get faster in the ways that matter on court — explode off the first step and beat your man both ways.

  • Running Speed — top-end speed to win transition
  • Sprint Mechanics — efficient form so every stride counts
  • Acceleration — building speed from a dead stop
  • First-Step Quickness — the explosive step that beats defenders
  • Defensive Speed — slides, closeouts and lateral quickness to lock up
  • Change Of Direction — decelerate, cut and re-accelerate at speed
