What they'll learn

The basics,
done properly.

Little Zeros is the place for young players to learn the fundamentals the right way and fall in love with the game along the way.

01

Dribble

Control the ball with both hands, keep their head up and move with it.

02

Pass

Chest, bounce and overhead passes, and how to find a teammate.

03

Shoot

Good shooting form from the start, plus layups off both feet.

04

Move

Footwork, balance and coordination so they move like a basketballer.

New to basketball?

Start here.
Start from zero.

Little Zeros is built for kids under 10, whether they've never picked up a ball or they already play and want better habits. Our under 10s program is where young players pick up the fundamentals and build the confidence to take them onto the court.

Sessions are run by the From Zero coaching team, led by Emmanuel Malou, who played professionally for 12 years, and Jimmy, who has spent 12 years coaching players from grassroots through to the pros.

  • Fun, structured sessions with a plan every week
  • Skills taught the right way from day one
  • Confidence on the ball and on the court
