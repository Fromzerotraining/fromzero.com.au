The Structure

12 Weeks,
4 Phases

A tendon doesn't heal by resting and hoping. It rebuilds under the right load, in the right order. Every phase has a job — and a physio gate before you progress.

Phase 1Weeks 1–3
Settle & Isometric

Calm the pain and load the tendon safely. Isometric holds reduce symptoms while we establish your baseline VALD numbers and movement screen — so every decision from here is measured, not guessed.

Phase 2Weeks 4–6
Heavy Slow Resistance

The evidence-backed core of tendon rehab. Heavy, slow, controlled strength work remodels the tendon and rebuilds capacity through the knee, quad and posterior chain.

Phase 3Weeks 7–9
Energy Storage

Reintroduce spring. Plyometrics, jump-landing mechanics and progressive loading teach the tendon to absorb and release force — the exact demand basketball puts on it.

Phase 4Weeks 10–12
Return To Court

Sport-specific volume, change of direction and on-court integration. Final VALD testing confirms you've closed the gap and are cleared to compete — stronger than when the pain started.

Physio Gate Every 3 Weeks Our sports physio reviews you at each phase gate. You only progress when the knee is ready — no rushing, no guessing.
VALD At Baseline, Mid & Final Objective force-plate data tracks strength, symmetry and explosiveness. Your return-to-play decision is backed by numbers, not a hunch.
